## 3. The gap

What every one of them misses: a direct, math-driven "is it worth it AT YOUR sub count" answer. Uscreen hedges ("it depends"), Milx overclaims ("best decision ever made") without honest downside weighting, Mediacube gives per-tier payout math but never ties it to realistic conversion rate in one combined worked example. None combine (a) correct current eligibility, (b) realistic 1-5% conversion, and (c) per-tier revenue math into one number a reader can apply to themselves.

What we can answer from our own data that they structurally cannot: nothing database-driven, this is a guide article (like `youtube-monetization-beyond-ads`), not a data study. The differentiator is synthesis and accuracy (correct current eligibility numbers, several competitors have stale ones), not proprietary data.

Intent the SERP does not close: reader wants "should I turn this on, yes or no, given my channel size" — most ranking pages explain the feature well but stop short of a usable verdict.
