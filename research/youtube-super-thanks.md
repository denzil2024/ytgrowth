## 3. The gap

What every one of them misses: **the real net payout is not always 70%.** Every vendor-content competitor states the 30% YouTube cut as the whole story. Real creator reports on Reddit show a second, separate cut on top when a viewer pays via an iOS device (Apple's own ~30% platform fee), which can drop a creator's real take closer to 50% of what the viewer paid, not 70%. None of the 5 competitors fetched mention this at all.

What we can answer from real sourcing that they can't: nothing database-driven (same guide-article category as the memberships spoke, per `feedback-goal-before-title-process` and the established cluster pattern), but the Apple-tax finding is real, verifiable, and currently absent from every ranking page.

Intent the SERP does not close: a reader who already got a Super Thanks and is confused why the payout looked lower than expected. That's a real, recurring, unanswered question based on the Reddit thread titles alone.

## 10. Real eligibility correction found during gap research, 2026-09-02

Third research pass surfaced a genuine error, not just a coverage gap: both this article's original Eligibility section AND the already-published `youtube-channel-memberships` article's Eligibility section incorrectly stated fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships) requires the STANDARD 1,000-subscriber YPP tier.

**The real, verified structure** (confirmed via YouTube's own creator page, blog.youtube's "YouTube Partner Program Explained" post, and cross-checked against multiple independent sources):
- **Lower "early access" tier: 500 subscribers** + (3,000 public watch hours in 365 days OR 3 million Shorts views in 90 days) + 3 public uploads. Unlocks **fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships) and Shopping**.
- **Standard tier: 1,000 subscribers** + (4,000 watch hours OR 10 million Shorts views). Required only for **ad revenue sharing**.

This matches a claim already correctly stated in the live `youtube-monetization-beyond-ads` pillar (line ~11192: "Early-access tier: a lower bar (around 500 subscribers...) that unlocks fan funding and channel memberships first"), which I had not cross-checked against when writing the memberships spoke originally. Also matches Veefly's "500 subscribers" claim from the first research pass, which I incorrectly dismissed as unverified at the time, it was correct, I just hadn't found the right official source yet.

**Fixed in both articles** (this Super Thanks article before its first publish, and retroactively in the already-live `youtube-channel-memberships` article's Eligibility section and closing paragraph).

**Lesson:** when a competitor or sibling article's claim conflicts with what I've verified, don't assume the competitor is wrong just because the first official source I checked didn't confirm it. Check a second and third official source, especially for a claim that changes reader-facing advice (telling someone to wait for a bar they don't actually need to clear is a real, actionable error, not a cosmetic one).
